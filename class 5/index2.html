Chicken Biryani

Time: 1 hour | Serves: 8 people

Ingredients

  • Rice
    • Basmati Rice - 500 grams
  • Meat
    • Chicken - 2 kg
    • Yogurt - 1 cup
  • Spices
    • Cardamom, Cinnamon, Cloves
    • Biryani Masala - 4 teaspoons

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak the rice for 30 minutes
  2. Marinate the meat with yogurt and spices
  3. Cook the meat
  4. Boil the rice until 90% cooked
  5. Layer the rice and meat, then cook on dum for 15 minutes
